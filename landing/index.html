How it works

1. Auto-Scan

Bypasses portal 504 timeouts to scrape all pending worksheets and MCQs across every course.

2. AI Personas

Generates contextual answers using LLMs configured to match your specific writing style and tone.

3. Drive Sync & Submit

Fills the native .docx, uploads it to your Google Drive via OAuth, and submits the link to the portal.
