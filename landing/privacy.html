Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

Quill ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our desktop application collects, uses, and handles your data.

1. Local-Only Data Storage

Quill is a native desktop application. We do not host any central servers or databases. All configuration data, university credentials, AI personas, and cached files are stored strictly locally on your machine in the ~/.quill/ directory.

2. Google Drive API Usage

Quill requests access to your Google Drive via the drive.file OAuth scope. This access is used exclusively to:

  • Create a /Quill folder in your Google Drive.
  • Upload locally generated .docx worksheet files into this specific folder.
  • Generate a public sharing link for those specific uploaded files to submit to your university portal.

Quill cannot read, modify, or delete any other files in your Google Drive. We do not transmit your Google Drive data to any third-party servers. Your OAuth token (token.pickle) is saved locally on your machine.

3. AI Provider Usage

To generate answers, Quill sends the text of your worksheet questions to an AI Provider (LLM API). By using Quill, you consent to sending this worksheet context to the configured LLM endpoint.

4. Telemetry and Tracking

Quill contains zero analytics, telemetry, or crash reporting. We do not know who you are, how often you use the app, or what files you generate.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please open an issue on our GitHub Repository.
